Sample Itinerary

Himalayan Escape

A sample 7-day journey through Bhutan.

DAY 1

Arrival & Welcome

Arrive in Paro, meet your guide, and settle in before a gentle introduction to Bhutan.

DAY 2

Cultural Exploration

Explore Paro town and visit the National Museum and nearby historic landmarks.

DAY 3

Mountain Journey

Drive to Thimphu through mountain scenery, with stops at viewpoints and villages along the way.

DAY 4

Monastery Adventure

Hike to the cliffside Tiger's Nest Monastery (Paro Taktsang) with time to rest along the trail.

DAY 5

Local Experience

Visit Punakha Dzong and enjoy Bhutanese dishes and a walk through the surrounding valley.

DAY 6

Nature & Relaxation

Spend a peaceful day in the Phobjikha Valley, known for its wide-open scenery and wintering black-necked cranes.

DAY 7

Departure

Breakfast, final exploration, and transfer to the airport.
